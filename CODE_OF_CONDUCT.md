# Code of Conduct

We want sidecar to be a friendly place to learn from each other's devices and code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to networking or to
  contributing.
- Assume good faith, and give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy: don't share other people's personal data, device identifiers or
  network details.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent
- Requests for, or sharing of, ways to use sidecar against devices or networks you
  don't own
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run for the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions, and may block people
who don't follow this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's private report option on
the offending content, or open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled privately.
